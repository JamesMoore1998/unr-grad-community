Resources

A directory of resources useful to graduate students at UNR, organized by category. Nothing here is invented — entries without a single verified official link say so plainly, instead of guessing.

Graduate School

Admissions, degree requirements, forms, funding, and Graduate School–wide policies.

Graduate School website Admissions, programs, policies, and Graduate School news.
Degree requirements & forms Program of study, graduation, and other Graduate School forms.
Assistantships & funding Assistantships, fellowships, and other funding for graduate students.

Academic Resources

Writing support, libraries, advising, and general academic help.

University Libraries Books, journals, research guides, and librarian appointments.
University Writing & Speaking Center Free one-on-one consultations on writing and speaking, at any stage.
University Tutoring Center Free tutoring appointments and drop-in sessions.
Academic advising University-wide advising hub; graduate advising is typically handled within your own college or department.

Research & Seminars

Department seminars, colloquia, and other research-related opportunities.

Department seminar series These vary by department and aren't centralized — check with your own department or program.
Research & Proposal Development Services Help finding funding and developing research proposals.
Sponsored Projects Administers externally funded research grants and agreements.

Professional Development

Career services, teaching resources, and skill-building opportunities.

Nevada Career Studio Central career services office, open to undergraduate and graduate students.
Graduate assistant handbook Covers GTA training requirements (GRAD 701S) and assistantship expectations.

Campus Services

Health & wellness, transportation, housing, and other services on campus.

Student Health Center Medical care for enrolled students.
Counseling Services Free, confidential psychological services for students.
Parking & Transportation Services Permits, alternative transportation, and the Ed-Pass bus program.
Graduate Student Housing On-campus housing options for eligible graduate students, including Ponderosa Village.

Student Organizations

Graduate student associations and department-level organizations.

Graduate Student Association (GSA) Represents graduate students; hosts events, awards, and funding programs.
Department & program organizations Vary by department — ask the GSA or your program if one exists. Suggest one to add it here.

Community & Social

Ways to meet other graduate students outside of coursework and research.

Recurring social events See the Events page for the shared calendar.

New Graduate Students

Starting points for students who are new to UNR or new to Reno.

New Graduate Student Orientation Held each fall; presentations, discussions, and recorded materials.
Office of International Students and Scholars (OISS) Admissions, visa, and transition support for international students.
Moving to Reno Not written yet — if you have advice on housing, transit, or cost of living, suggest it.
